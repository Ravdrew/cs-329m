Skip configured properties that do not exist on the Meeting object. Resolve each configured value by its declared type and source: literal text, booking-field placeholders, UTM placeholders, booleans, phone values, or booking dates. Support placeholders within surrounding text as well as a value consisting entirely of one placeholder, and define a consistent empty-value behavior when the requested booking data is unavailable.

Convert dates and other typed values to the representation expected by the existing HubSpot integration before merging the mapped properties into the create request.
